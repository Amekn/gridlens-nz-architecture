# GridLens NZ — Design 1 requirements

**Artifact version:** 0.5 approved
**Status:** Approved at Gate 1
**Approval evidence:** User message, "Approve Gate 1 v0.5"
**Change source:** User messages requiring developer-managed demo credentials, direct region selection on the map, visible markers, and near-production quality
**Companion:** `00-idea-brief.md` version 0.4 draft
**Normative base:** `Shared/GridLens NZ.md`, as amended by the explicit user directions and Daniel transcript integration recorded in `00-idea-brief.md`

## Goals

- Provide a whole-New-Zealand, map-led early assessment of proposed data-centre People and Planet impacts.
- Keep engineering calculations, assessment outcomes, provenance states, and safeguards deterministic and reproducible.
- Make an operator-configured LLM/MCP research agent available without exposing provider credentials or configuration work to ordinary users, and without giving the agent authority over deterministic results.
- Ground live and prepared evidence in multiple official/public sources with explicit authority, licence, spatial coverage, freshness, and conflict handling.
- Serve both public and decision-maker needs without changing the underlying record.
- Preserve Design 1 as an isolated OpenAI Sites project with a map-first browser UI, narrowly scoped same-origin agent/research routes, server-only provider secrets, and no user database.

## Requirement conventions

- **Must** is required for the Gate 1 MVP.
- **Should** is expected unless architecture or test evidence shows a disproportionate cost.
- **Could** is stretch scope and does not block the core release.
- “Authoritative” means authoritative for the specific field and time period, not generally trustworthy for every inference.
- “Whole-NZ support” means all defined geographies can be selected and receive deterministic calculations; evidence depth may vary and must be disclosed per category.

## Deterministic calculation and assessment requirements

| ID | Observable requirement | Priority | Acceptance summary |
|---|---|---:|---|
| FR-CAL-001 | Facility demand shall equal `IT capacity × PUE`; annual energy shall equal `facility MW × utilisation × 8,760 ÷ 1,000`. | Must | 50 MW, PUE 1.3, utilisation 0.8 yields exactly 65 MW and 455.52 GWh before formatting. |
| FR-CAL-002 | Flexible load shall be calculated from total facility demand. The simplified 24-hour simulation shall preserve daily work unless a later approved scope explicitly introduces curtailment. | Must | 65 MW at 30% produces a 19.5 MW upper bound and energy conservation holds within defined tolerance. |
| FR-CAL-003 | The simulator shall deterministically minimise the maximum regional-plus-facility load while respecting flexible-load, hourly headroom, destination-utilisation, original-combined-peak, and same-day bounds. | Must | The frozen 100/50/50 MW counterexample shifts 25 MWh to each destination and reduces the peak from 150 MW to 125 MW. |
| FR-CAL-004 | Water use shall be an indicative range derived from facility demand, utilisation, cooling method, and versioned factors. Unknown cooling or absent factor data shall return insufficient evidence, not zero or a fabricated midpoint. | Must | Each supported cooling method returns a labelled range; unknown returns no numeric estimate. |
| FR-CAL-005 | Every result shall retain formula/algorithm version, exact normalized inputs and units, unrounded and display values, assumptions, region/geometry/profile/factor/threshold/evidence/policy versions, and retrieval `as-of` time where applicable. | Must | “Show calculation” exposes a complete reproducibility manifest. |
| FR-CAL-006 | Each result shall independently report `complete`, `insufficient evidence`, or `failed`; one failure shall not remove unaffected results. | Must | A water-factor failure leaves electricity and flexibility outputs visible. |
| FR-ASM-001 | Electricity, water, resilience, economic contribution, and community categories shall deterministically return `low concern`, `moderate concern`, `high concern`, or `insufficient evidence`, with reason IDs and traced inputs/evidence. | Must | No missing decisive input defaults to low. |
| FR-ASM-002 | Electricity concern shall compare post-flexibility added combined peak with regional peak: `≤5%` low, `>5% and ≤15%` moderate, `>15%` high. Low additionally requires current authoritative demand and grid evidence; missing required evidence yields insufficient evidence. | Must | Exact 5% and 15% boundaries, stale evidence, and missing profiles are tested. |
| FR-ASM-003 | Water concern shall require current authoritative regional water evidence and source-backed absolute annual/peak thresholds. The conservative upper water estimate shall be compared; cooling efficiency alone cannot produce low concern. | Must | A low WUE factor without regional thresholds returns insufficient evidence. |
| FR-ASM-004 | Low resilience concern shall require verified battery or demand-response capacity, duration, and commitment. Restricted fossil backup may be moderate; an explicit user assertion of unrestricted fossil backup may produce a conservative high outcome traced to that assumption; unknown backup is insufficient. | Must | Boolean labels alone cannot create low concern and the fossil-assumption branch is explicit. |
| FR-ASM-005 | Economic contribution shall assess evidence robustness, not economic desirability. Current independent evidence with role/basis detail may be low; unsupported developer/user claims are moderate; high requires exact contradictory current authoritative evidence. Jobs above 10,000 is a suspicion warning/moderate unsupported claim, and no investment high threshold is invented. | Must | All origin and contradiction branches are tested. |
| FR-ASM-006 | Community concern shall be insufficient without representative consultation evidence. Current cited concerns can support moderate/high outcomes. Web/news sentiment or a small voluntary response set shall never be labelled representative and cannot produce low concern. | Must | Search-result counts and AI sentiment alone cannot determine community concern. |
| FR-ASM-007 | Overall narrative precedence shall be: any high → substantial issues require investigation; otherwise any insufficient → evidence incomplete; otherwise any moderate → potentially suitable with conditions; all low → lower-concern scenario still requiring professional review. | Must | Every category combination resolves deterministically without a score or approval/rejection. |
| FR-ASM-008 | The product shall not compute a single suitability score, claim a “best” site, issue formal approval/rejection, or make a legal, engineering, cultural, environmental, or consent determination. | Must | UI, exports, agent instructions, and tested responses contain no forbidden authoritative recommendation. |

## Evidence and public-data requirements

| ID | Observable requirement | Priority | Acceptance summary |
|---|---|---:|---|
| FR-EVD-001 | Every evidence record shall include stable ID, title, publisher, publication/observation time, retrieval time, optional validity end, URL/resource ID, source adapter, source type, evidence category, geography/geometry, licence/attribution, relevant extract, quality/authority classification, and derivation state. | Must | Schema validation rejects missing required provenance and incompatible units/geography. |
| FR-EVD-002 | Evidence shall distinguish authoritative dataset, official document, developer/proponent claim, independent analysis, regulator/court finding, news report, community submission, web-discourse indicator, user assumption, prepared preset, calculation, and AI interpretation. | Must | Each representative item has one visible type and cannot silently change type. |
| FR-EVD-003 | Freshness shall be evaluated against a pinned result `as-of`: 24 months for electricity, grid, water, resilience, employment, investment, and community/current-news evidence; 36 months for cooling, planning, legislation-context, and environmental evidence. An explicit `validUntil` overrides the default; missing date is `unknown freshness`. | Must | Exact boundary days, future dates, validity override, and unknown dates are tested independently of browser clock. |
| FR-EVD-004 | Stale or unknown-freshness evidence shall remain visible but cannot satisfy a low-concern precondition. Broken links or unavailable feeds shall be disclosed, not removed from history. | Must | A low outcome cannot rely on stale/unknown evidence. |
| FR-EVD-005 | Each enabled source shall have a versioned registry entry specifying publisher authority, supported fields, access mode, authentication, expected CORS behavior, licence/attribution, permitted purpose, quota, refresh cadence, spatial/temporal resolution, and fallback. | Must | Build/runtime validation rejects an unregistered source or a use outside its declared purpose. |
| FR-EVD-006 | Direct runtime browser calls shall be made only to explicit HTTPS endpoints that pass a real CORS compatibility check for the required method and headers. A non-CORS source may only be a versioned build-time import, user-opened official link, or provider-side read-only research source; the app shall not silently proxy it. | Must | Electricity Authority blob and NZ Legislation examples follow their declared non-direct paths when CORS is absent. |
| FR-EVD-007 | Live data shall be treated as contextual unless the assessment policy explicitly declares the source/field authoritative. Live values shall retain observation time, retrieval time, unit, geography, and source status. | Must | A current price or carbon-intensity value cannot establish long-term capacity or suitability. |
| FR-EVD-008 | EM6 anonymous feeds shall be retained for live price, reserve, carbon-intensity, and intermittent-generation context where applicable. EM6 node/grid-zone data shall be mapped explicitly and shall not be represented as a regional-council measurement without a valid mapping. | Must | An unmapped or ambiguous EM6 record stays separate from regional assessment inputs. |
| FR-EVD-009 | Stronger sources shall complement rather than silently overwrite EM6 or other sources. Material conflicts shall show both values, definitions, timestamps, geographies, and an unresolved/conflict status unless a deterministic precedence rule applies. | Must | A conflicting EA/EM6 fixture is visible and reproducible. |
| FR-EVD-010 | Whole-NZ analysis shall use a national prepared fallback only when a qualifying regional value is unavailable; it shall be labelled `national context`, lower the relevant coverage state, and never be described as local fact. | Must | A fallback retains national source provenance and cannot satisfy regional-evidence gates. |
| FR-EVD-011 | The first source set shall support the registry surveyed in `00-idea-brief.md`: Stats NZ, LINZ, Transpower, Electricity Authority/EMI, EM6, LAWA, NIWA, GeoNet, Data.govt.nz, EECA, MfE, NZTA, NZ Legislation, NZBN/Companies Office where permitted, EPA/council official documents, and Tavily. Individual adapters may be disabled until terms, schema, and CORS tests pass. | Should | The shipped source manifest says `enabled`, `prepared-only`, `link-only`, `agent-only`, or `disabled` with a reason. |
| FR-EVD-012 | A device-local research cache shall retain query, connector, result/evidence IDs, retrieval time, source URLs, freshness, and schema/version data; users can refresh, inspect, or clear it. Cached content shall never be presented as newly retrieved after expiry. | Should | Reload preserves cached research and marks stale records without changing their original retrieval time. |
| FR-EVD-013 | The prepared electricity pipeline shall prefer Electricity Authority EMI structured data over report-page scraping: subscribed real-time dispatch API when configured and CORS-compatible; otherwise anonymous Azure Blob REST files. It shall ingest the latest complete rolling 12 months of daily `DispatchNodalPricesAndVolumes` plus the latest compatible current NSP table, with `Generation_MD` used only as a separately labelled legacy plant/fuel cross-reference when needed. | Must | The manifest records every raw URL, ETag/last-modified, checksum, schema, coverage interval, missing day, and source caveat; a public report-page DOM is never the data contract. |
| FR-EVD-014 | EMI spatial aggregation shall exact-join `PointOfConnectionCode`/`POC_Code`, transform valid NSP NZTM coordinates (`EPSG:2193`) to WGS84, and assign a Stats NZ region by the same deterministic point-in-polygon rule as the map. The NSP `Network reporting region` shall remain a separate electricity-network dimension. Missing/ambiguous coordinates or unmatched POCs shall be reported and excluded from geographic aggregation unless a versioned authoritative mapping resolves them. | Must | Coordinate fixtures and known nodes land in the expected region; unmatched rows are counted and never assigned to the nearest node/region. |
| FR-EVD-015 | EMI interval processing shall preserve source semantics and New Zealand offsets, handle 46/48/50 half-hour daylight-saving days where applicable, distinguish dispatch/modelled generation from metered/estimated plant output, retain MW versus kWh units, and publish coverage/quality metrics. Regional generation/output shall be labelled context and shall not be interpreted as spare connection capacity, regional self-sufficiency, or transmission headroom. | Must | DST, unit, duplicate-run, disconnected/dead-node, and missing-interval fixtures aggregate without double counting or false capacity claims. |
| FR-EVD-016 | Project evidence shall form a typed, versioned graph with at least claim → source → publication/observation date, project → company → site, concern → stakeholder/evidence, project → comparable, document → page/section, and contradiction/supersession edges. AI may propose edges, but validation and provenance state remain deterministic. | Must | The demo discrepancy traces both conflicting claims to exact source/page/date records and never creates a fact from an unsupported edge. |
| FR-EVD-017 | Community evidence shall record stance (`supportive`, `opposed`, `conditional`, `neutral/informational`, `unclear`), controlled themes, stakeholder context, evidence quality/method, date, geography, and source. Mana whenua/iwi/hapū evidence shall remain a distinct category and shall not be merged into a general public-sentiment percentage. | Must | A mixed-source fixture shows missing voices and method limits; no 63%-positive style output is generated without a qualifying representative survey. |
| FR-EVD-018 | Each prepared project shall have a source-backed timeline covering applicable announcements, applications, hearings, infrastructure agreements, commitments, objections/appeals, capacity/design changes, and evidence updates. Conflicting dates shall remain separate and be flagged rather than silently reconciled. | Must | The case file can show a documented project date versus a later infrastructure date as a cited discrepancy. |

## Quality requirements

| ID | Observable requirement | Acceptance summary |
|---|---|---|

## Explicit non-goals

- Formal approval/rejection, consent outcome, legal advice, engineering capacity study, cultural impact assessment, environmental impact assessment, or professional site-selection recommendation.
- A numeric overall suitability/ethics/sentiment score or an autonomous “best site.”
- Precise household electricity-price prediction or a claim that spot/regional prices equal consumer bills.
- A claim that web/news sentiment is representative public consultation.
- Arbitrary AI-generated HTML/code execution or unapproved MCP actions.
- A national resource-consent scraper where no official uniform API/permission exists.
- Use of Broadband Map NZ’s availability API for this research purpose under its currently published restrictions.
- User accounts, server-side collaboration/sharing, app-owned scenario persistence, or user-provided credentials/endpoints in Design 1.
- User-supplied proposal/PDF upload and direct public-feedback collection as core release blockers. Prepared documents for the deep demonstration case remain core.
